Prepared by the 30th Anniversary production team · October 2026

The documentary tells the thirty years of The Life Church in order, in five parts, from Nairobi to the years ahead. Since July, nineteen people have been interviewed in eight cities on four continents. Pastors John and Leslie’s interview ties those stories together and covers the parts no one else can tell.

Nearly every interview ends with words about Pastors John and Leslie. Those are left out of this document so they can be heard first in the film.

Quotes are taken word for word from the interview transcripts. The film’s structure is a working draft and may change in the edit.

The plan for October 8

The interview follows the film’s five parts, in order. Each part lists who has already covered it on film and the topics the interview will cover. Dates still to confirm are noted where they come up.

Opening

The film’s first two minutes

Pastor John’s voiceover from the script opens the film. It plays over a scene of Pastors John and Leslie entering the building, going into the green room, meeting members of the team, and looking over the service flows for the morning. That scene is filmed on a separate day.

Sent

Nairobi · 1993–1996

The church was born on a mission field. One voice carries these years: Pastor Don Matheny, whose church sent Pastors John and Leslie to Memphis.

Planted

Memphis · 1996–2002

Seven people, a rented room, and six years of setting up and tearing down, until a derelict grocery store on Germantown Parkway.

On filmPete and Kristi Sciacchetano, Rob Carter

  • Why Memphis, a city where they knew almost no one, with Nairobi Lighthouse supporting them through the search and after the launch.

III

Multiplied

The city and the next generation · 2002–2017

Katrina sends the church out of the building. Axis grows from thirty students to a conference, the Serve Trucks roll out to the schools, the church spreads to new locations, and Santiago becomes the first church outside the United States.

On filmPastors Mundo Meneses, Dan Lord, Johnny Hill, Parker VanBlaricom, Adam Bartlett, and Josh and Jessica Fuentes, with Brian Pitre on the TV years.

  • The big steps of faith: Germantown Parkway, the Collierville YMCA, Houston Levee and its auditorium, and why each one was taken.
  • Katrina: why the church went to the Gulf Coast, and the people who came to the church through it.
  • The next generation: the prophecy of “restless young men and women,” and Axis.
  • Why television: the weekly program, 2008 to 2018.
  • The Sunday the first Serve Truck was announced, in 2011.
  • Santiago: what Pastor John felt for Chile, and the first location outside the United States, launched on his birthday, May 25, 2014.
  • Zoe: Pastor Leslie on the story of the women’s conference. It comes up on film but has not yet been told.

To confirmWhen the Collierville YMCA location began and ended.

IV

Family

Pastors to pastors · 2017–2021

Two couples who led their own churches asked Pastors John and Leslie to be their pastors before anything was merged. Then COVID.

On filmPastors Wayne and Classy Francis, Pastor Emy Vazquez, with Brian Pitre on March 2020 in Memphis

  • Becoming a family of churches: why they said yes to New York and Massachusetts, and later Florence and Johannesburg, and what it means to pastor those pastors.
  • March 2020 in Memphis: moving every service online in a single week, and Pastor John’s daily livestream updates with guests on Zoom, so the church stayed connected and no one was left uncared for.
V

Just Getting Started

2020–2027

The founding story, happening again: a lemon house in Florence, a first Sunday in Johannesburg, a school and a building still being built, and the church that sent Pastors John and Leslie, now joining the family.

The interviews so far

Fourteen interviews, in the order the film tells the story.

I · Sent

Pastor Don Matheny

Nairobi Lighthouse Church, Kenya · filmed in Nairobi, July 2026

Pastor Don met Pastor John on a missions team from Baton Rouge and took him into the Matheny home when he asked to stay. He recalls Pastors John and Leslie serving on staff for about three and a half years, their call to plant a church in America, the commissioning at City Stadium where the church pledged monthly support, and the call from Memphis in 1996: “we found the city.”

II · Planted

Pete and Kristi Sciacchetano

Friends since childhood, with the church from its first years · filmed in Memphis, September 2026

Pete met Pastor John in 1976, when they were boys in Louisiana, and Kristi met Pastor Leslie in sixth or seventh grade. They talk about the seven-person launch team, launch Sunday at the Home Builders Association, moving to Memphis “two Marches later,” and the church-in-a-box years, from tube TVs hauled up homemade ramps to baptisms in their hot tub.

Rob Carter

Member since the Home Builders Association days · filmed in Memphis, September 2026

Rob came to Memphis with FedEx and, after a friend insisted he visit, was “completely hooked right from the start.” He remembers weekly setup at the Home Builders Association, finding the old Mega Market on North Germantown Parkway, and Pastor John’s answer when Rob worried they were reaching too far: “We’re not reaching far enough.”

III · Multiplied

Pastor Mundo Meneses

Outreach and missions, with the church for more than 20 years · filmed in Memphis

Pastor Mundo describes outreach that began before the church launched (“not only have church, but be the church”): Read to Lead, Super Saturday, the COVID food lines, and the Serve Trucks, from the Sunday the first one was announced in 2011. He tells of the word Pastors John and Leslie received after Nairobi, that God would replace them “200 times over on the mission field,” and of Nairobi Lighthouse Church joining The Life Church family.

Pastor Dan Lord

On staff 2005–2018, built Axis and the Axis Conference, now pastoring in St. Louis · filmed in St. Louis, August 2026

Pastor Dan met Pastors John and Leslie at a conference in 2004 and moved his family to Memphis with no job description. His first assignments were the nursery and Katrina relief. He tells how Axis grew from 30 or 40 students to the first Axis Conference in 2009, and about the next-gen culture behind it, from Tribes to the “I’m a leader” chant.

Pastor Johnny Hill

Worship, with the church since Hurricane Katrina · filmed in Memphis, September 2026

Pastor Johnny evacuated New Orleans for Hurricane Katrina in 2005, heard Pastor John on the radio talking about the church’s relief project, and came to a service that night. Within weeks he was on the worship team, and today he oversees worship in Memphis and across the locations. He talks about worship for “participators, not spectators” and writing songs that serve the church.

Pastor Parker VanBlaricom

Next Gen and the Axis Conference, in the church since he was eight · filmed in Memphis, September 2026

Pastor Parker’s family found the church in the Yellow Pages in August 2000, when he was eight. He joined Axis as a seventh grader in 2005, was at the first Axis Conference in 2009 (about 200 to 250 students), and took Axis over from Pastor Dan in 2018. The conference has since grown to more than 2,000 students, sold out. He also recalls a prophecy Pastor John has often spoken of, that God would send the church “restless young men and women.”

Pastor Adam Bartlett

The church’s building projects, on staff since 2008 · filmed in Memphis, September 2026

Pastor Adam’s first Sunday was June 10, 2007, and he joined the staff in April 2008. He tells the story behind each building: the Germantown Parkway renovations, church in the gym at the Collierville YMCA, Houston Levee (opened in February 2010), Highland (September 2014), the 2,000-seat auditorium, buildings in New York and Fitchburg, and the NextGen Global Hub, due around September 2027.

Pastors Josh and Jessica Fuentes

The Life Church Santiago, Chile · filmed in Santiago, August 2026, partly in Spanish

After word came that Pastor John “had felt something for Chile,” Pastors Josh and Jessica moved their honeymoon from Europe to Memphis. The Life Church Santiago launched on May 25, 2014, Pastor John’s birthday, with a team of 13 and about 200 people. After twelve years as a portable church, it now meets every Sunday at the Ritz-Carlton, with a second location planned for 2027.

Brian Pitre

The Life Church production team · filmed in Memphis, October 2026

Brian tells how the church readied the Germantown Parkway auditorium in 2007 for Pastor John’s vision of a local TV program. It launched in January 2008 on three local stations and became the most-watched local program in Memphis, each episode ending with a fresh start moment Pastor John filmed for the viewer. He also describes March 2020, when that experience moved the church online in one week, with a live update from Pastor John every night so that “nobody was forgotten.”

IV · Family

Pastors Wayne and Classy Francis

The Life Church New York · filmed in Westchester, August 2026

Pastors Wayne and Classy planted Authentic Church in Westchester in 2009 and met Pastors John and Leslie at ARC in 2017. They asked them to be their pastors, announced the merger in January 2020, and launched online that Easter as COVID reached New York. They also talk about Black Fist White Knuckles, the former CVS that became their building, and 5,000 people over nine services on Easter 2026.

Pastor Emy Vazquez

The Life Church Massachusetts · filmed in Fitchburg, August 2026

Pastor Emy came to faith in prison and planted his church through eight or nine venues. A recurring dream about “partnership” led him to call Pastor Wayne, then Pastor John, and the church became The Life Church Massachusetts. He talks about moving into a new building in Fitchburg in February 2025, a cancer diagnosis in the same season (he is now cancer-free), and Axis growing from six to eight students to 185.

V · Just Getting Started

Pastors Marco and Tiina Geraci

The Life Church Italia, Florence · filmed in Florence, September 2026

Pastors Marco and Tiina met in London and followed a call to Florence. Introduced to Pastors John and Leslie through Will Johnson and Pastor Mundo, they launched in January 2022 in an unheated lemon house with 90 people. They talk about pastoring in a Catholic country, the English-speaking service Pastor John suggested, 2,076 salvations so far, and a dream of Zoe Europe.

Pastors Adrian and Leigh Wright

The Life Church South Africa, Johannesburg · filmed in Johannesburg, September 2026

Pastors Adrian and Leigh planted their Johannesburg church in 2015 with sixteen people. After meeting Pastors John and Leslie in Memphis and a year of conversations, they held their first Sunday as The Life Church South Africa on March 3, 2024, and have grown about 30% a year since. Adrian also tells how the Life Leadership Bible School began, from a mandate Pastor John gave him in Nashville in June 2024, and reached 400 students in 11 countries in its first year.
